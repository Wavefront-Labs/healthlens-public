Common questions

How do I upload a document?

From the Records tab, tap the add button and either take a photo or choose a file. HealthLens reads it, files it, and folds it into your AI-generated summary automatically.

Is my data stored on your servers?

No. Your health records live on your device. Documents are processed transiently to generate your report and are then discarded — see our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up or move my data to a new device?

Settings → Export creates a portable backup file you control. Use Settings → Import on your new device to restore it.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through the App Store or Google Play. Manage or cancel anytime via your device's subscription settings (iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions; Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions).

How do I delete my account or data?

Since your records live on your device, deleting the app removes your health data. To close your account entirely, email support@health-lens.com.

Is HealthLens a medical device or a source of medical advice?

No. HealthLens is a personal health record organiser. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions, and never use HealthLens in an emergency — call 000 (AU) or 111 (NZ).

Report a bug or safety issue

Email support@health-lens.com with as much detail as you can — what happened, what you expected, and your device/app version if known.
